# Portfolio templates

Ready-to-copy portfolios for [People pages on SLAPorSLOP](https://slaporslop.games/people). Your People page
is your shop window: who you are, your style, your best work and your rates. The portfolio is the
"best work" part, and it lives in a public GitHub repository of your own.

| Template | For | Works |
|---|---|---|
| [gallery](gallery/) | Illustration, concept art, pixel art | Pictures |
| [showreel](showreel/) | Animation, motion, video | YouTube videos with posters, plus a picture |
| [tracklist](tracklist/) | Music and sound design | Audio tracks with covers, plus a video |
| [studio](studio/) | Teams, or one person offering several services | A mix |

Each folder is a whole portfolio repository: its `.slop/` folder passes every check SLAPorSLOP makes,
with placeholder pictures (and, in tracklist, two one-second test tones) for you to replace.
Each folder's README says what to change.

Each one also has a page design in `.slop/page/` (`index.html` and `style.css`), with its own look:
Gallery a big wall of pictures, Showreel one featured video, Tracklist a numbered track list, and
Studio the team and its services. Keep it, change it, or delete the `page/` folder for SLAPorSLOP's
standard layout; the rules are under "Your page's design" in the guide.

The full guide, with every rule: [portfolio.md](https://slaporslop.games/portfolio.md) (also on any
SLAPorSLOP site at `/portfolio.md`). Using an AI assistant? Tell it: *"Read
https://slaporslop.games/portfolio.md and help me build my SLAPorSLOP portfolio."*
