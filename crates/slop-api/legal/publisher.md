> **Draft for legal review.** This agreement hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer yet. You'll get 15 days' notice of any change.

This agreement is between you (the **maker**) and IronPoint Development LLC ("Slop"). It applies when you publish an app, a page or a branch on Slop, on top of the Terms of Service and the Content Policy.

## 1. You're the maker
- Your app is yours, and you're responsible for it: what it does, its updates, and complying with the law where it's offered. Slop is a directory and delivery service, not your publisher.
- Your store page names you as the maker. Keep a way for Slop to reach you.
- Slop builds and signs your app from your public source, but never changes your code or builds.

## 2. Your rights to what you publish
You promise that you have the rights to all code and assets in your app, including anything made with AI, and that you follow the licence of everything you include. Your app's source must be public under an open-source licence.

## 3. What apps can't do
Apps must follow the Content Policy. In particular:
- no in-app purchases, selling, loot boxes, paid random rewards or gambling;
- no malware, hidden data collection, or data theft;
- builds must match their source.

**AI:** you must follow the EU AI Act's transparency rules where they apply: tell users when they're talking to an AI, and label AI-made content where the law requires. Banned AI uses aren't allowed.

## 4. Scans and reviews
Slop checks releases before players get them, and may withdraw a release or take an app out of the store if it breaks the rules, with a statement of reasons and the right to appeal. If a release is dangerous, Slop can stop it from launching on players' computers (the kill switch).

## 5. Votes and reviews
Never offer anything in exchange for votes or reviews, and never ask players for good ones in return for something. Slop doesn't remove reviews because they're negative.

## 6. Donations (when they open)
Donations give nothing in return. Your app can't know who donated and must be the same for everyone. Slop's fee is 5% of each donation. Donations may be taxable income for you; Slop doesn't give tax advice.

## 7. Liability
You're responsible for claims caused by your app or by breaking this agreement, and you'll cover Slop's reasonable costs from them (to the extent the law allows).

## 8. Ending
You can take your app out of the store at any time. Slop may end this agreement for serious or repeated breaches, with a statement of reasons. Players who installed your app keep it.
