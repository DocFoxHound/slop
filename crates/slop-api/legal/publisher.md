This agreement is between you (the **maker**) and IronPoint Development LLC ("SLAPorSLOP"). It applies when you publish an app, a page or a branch on SLAPorSLOP, on top of the Terms of Service and the Content Policy.

## 1. You're the maker
- Your app is yours, and you're responsible for it: what it does, its updates, and complying with the law where it's offered. SLAPorSLOP is a directory and delivery service, not your publisher.
- Your store page names you as the maker. Keep a way for SLAPorSLOP to reach you.
- SLAPorSLOP builds and signs your app from your public source, but never changes your code or builds.

## 2. Your rights to what you publish
You promise that you have the rights to all code and assets in your app, including anything made with AI, and that you follow the licence of everything you include. Your app's source must be public under an open-source licence.

## 3. What apps can't do
Apps must follow the Content Policy. In particular:
- no in-app purchases, selling, loot boxes, paid random rewards or gambling;
- no malware, hidden data collection, or data theft;
- builds must match their source.

**AI:** you must follow the EU AI Act's transparency rules where they apply: tell users when they're talking to an AI, and label AI-made content where the law requires. Banned AI uses aren't allowed.

## 4. Scans and reviews
SLAPorSLOP checks releases before players get them, and may withdraw a release or take an app out of the store if it breaks the rules, with a statement of reasons and the right to appeal. If a release is dangerous, SLAPorSLOP can stop it from launching on players' computers (the kill switch).

## 5. Votes and reviews
Never offer anything in exchange for votes or reviews, and never ask players for good ones in return for something. SLAPorSLOP doesn't remove reviews because they're negative.

## 6. Donations (when they open)
Donations give nothing in return. Your app can't know who donated and must be the same for everyone. SLAPorSLOP's fee is 5% of each donation. Donations may be taxable income for you; SLAPorSLOP doesn't give tax advice.

**SLAPorSLOP's fee promise.** SLAPorSLOP's fee on money players give makers will never be more than 5%. SLAPorSLOP won't add a fee to publishing, listing, downloads or updates. Any other change to fees that costs makers more takes effect at least 12 months after SLAPorSLOP announces it, instead of the usual 15 days, and you can leave before then.

## 7. Liability
You're responsible for claims caused by your app or by breaking this agreement, and you'll cover SLAPorSLOP's reasonable costs from them (to the extent the law allows).

## 8. Ending
You can take your app out of the store at any time. SLAPorSLOP may end this agreement for serious or repeated breaches, with a statement of reasons. Players who installed your app keep it.

## 9. Leaving SLAPorSLOP
Your code and builds stay yours, on your own repository; SLAPorSLOP keeps no copies. Nothing in this agreement stops you publishing your app anywhere else as well. At any time you can download your account's data, your store pages and every release with SLAPorSLOP's signed manifests (Account → Download my data).
