This policy says what isn't allowed on SLAPorSLOP, in apps, store pages, reviews, profiles and messages, and how we act on it.

## How moderation works
- **Anyone can report** something with the Report button. You get a reference number.
- **People review reports.** Urgent reports (child abuse, threats to someone's life or safety, intimate images shared without consent) are handled first, within 24 hours. Other serious reports (malware, scams, illegal content, builds that don't match their source, apps that run code from outside SLAPorSLOP) are also reviewed within 24 hours. Intimate images are removed within 48 hours of a valid request at the latest.
- **What we can do:** take an app out of the store, hide a review or profile, remove a maker's reply under a review, warn someone, or suspend an account. We never hide a review because it's negative.
- **You're told why.** Each decision comes with a statement of reasons: what we did, the facts, the rule or law, whether automated tools were used, and how to appeal.
- **Appeals.** Within six months, from your account page. A person reviews the appeal and either keeps or reverses the decision.
- **Repeat breaches** lead to suspension. Three upheld copyright decisions end an account (our repeat-infringer policy).
- We report child sexual abuse material to the authorities, and threats to life to the police.

## No paid placement
Nobody can pay SLAPorSLOP, or anyone else, to rank higher, be featured, be recommended or appear on a shelf. The store's lists come from players' votes and play, staff picks made for players, and fair turns for new apps ("New and barely seen"), as described on "How ratings work". Donations, People jobs and hiring creatives never change where an app appears. Offering anything for votes, reviews or placement breaks the rules for everyone involved.

## The rules

### Malware and data theft
No malware, hidden crypto-mining, data theft, or collecting data without telling people.

### Builds must match their source
A build must be made from the source code it points to. Nothing hidden, added or swapped. Browser games have no build, so this rule doesn't cover them (see "Browser games and dedicated servers").

### Code from outside SLAPorSLOP
An app may only run code that came in a release or mod SLAPorSLOP checked. It mustn't download and run programs, libraries, scripts or mods from anywhere else, including its own servers or other players' servers, and mustn't update itself outside SLAPorSLOP. Mods must be published on SLAPorSLOP and approved there. Data the app reads with its own code (maps, levels, pictures, sounds, settings) may come from anywhere. This rule doesn't cover browser games or dedicated servers (see the next section).

### Browser games and dedicated servers
- **Browser games** play on their maker's own website instead of being downloaded. SLAPorSLOP checks the address before players see it and again whenever it changes, but not what the site serves. The rules on builds and on code from outside SLAPorSLOP don't apply to them. Every other rule does: the site must match its store page, mustn't sell anything in the game, and mustn't carry malware, scams, phishing or anything else this policy bans.
- **Dedicated servers** are programs people run on their own computer or a host so others can play a game. Each is published by the game's maker with the game it's for, and shown on that game's store page (some from before then are apps in the Servers category). A server may download and run mods, maps and plugins from other places, so the rule on code from outside SLAPorSLOP doesn't cover it. It's still built from its source and checked like any other release, and every other rule applies.

### Scams and fraud
No scams, phishing, impersonation or fake offers.

### Illegal content
Nothing illegal where SLAPorSLOP operates, including terrorist content.

### Child sexual abuse material
Banned in every form, including drawn and AI-made. Reported to the authorities.

### Intimate images shared without consent
No intimate images of anyone shared without their consent, including AI-made ones, and no "nudify" apps.

### Sexual content
Sexually explicit content isn't allowed on SLAPorSLOP.

### Threats and violence
No threats, and nothing that encourages violence or self-harm.

### Harassment, hate and bullying
No harassment, hate, or bullying of anyone.

### Sharing private information
Don't share other people's private information (addresses, phone numbers, documents) without their consent.

### Copyright and trademarks
Only post what you have the rights to. Copyright owners can send a notice through the Report button; the person it's about can send a counter-notice by appealing.

### Open-source licences
Apps on SLAPorSLOP are open source, and must follow the licences of everything they include.

### Pay-to-win, loot boxes and gambling
No gambling, loot boxes, paid random rewards, or anything sold inside an app.

### Misleading pages
Store pages and profiles must describe what they offer truthfully, including how AI was used.

### Fake, paid or off-topic reviews
Reviews must be honest and about the thing reviewed. Nobody may offer anything for a vote or a review.

### Reviews used as leverage
Threatening a bad review to get something not in the agreement isn't allowed.

### False statements of fact
Reviews may give any opinion, but mustn't state things that are false.

### Spam
No spam or repeated unwanted messages.
