**News from makers:** A maker's news posts show on their app's store page to anyone who can see that page. Only the maker sees a post we hid. When you mark an app Interested, we show you a notice under the bell when its maker posts news. We don't email these notices or send them to your device. To stop them for an app, switch off "Tell me about news from this app" on the app's page or in your library, or remove your Interested mark. If the maker removes the post, or we hide it, its notice goes too. The maker never sees who is told.

**Open requests are public:** If you post an open request on People, anyone can read it, signed in or not, with your display name and your project, until it closes. If you put your name forward for a request, only the person who posted it, and an AI agent they gave People access, sees that you did and when, with your card from the People directory (it links to your People page) and the package you named, if any. If you take it back, it leaves their list at once; we keep a note that you did until the request is deleted or you close your account, so they aren't told twice.

We never sell your data, and we don't use it to train AI models.

## Who receives it
Our hosting provider, GitHub (sign-in, apps' repositories), and Stripe (payments, as an independent controller). Makers never learn who donated unless the donor chooses to tell them.

**Makers:** A maker, and an AI agent they connected, see totals for their own apps: for each day, how many accounts played the app, how many played it for the first time, and how many times it was added to a library. They never see who. On a day when one account played, the total is 1. When these totals began, they were filled in once for the time before, from what was still stored: first plays of accounts that had "Share my playtime" on at that moment, and adds of apps that were still in a library. Since then, your launches count in these totals only while "Share my playtime" is on: to stay out of them, switch it off on your account page. What was already counted stays, since the totals hold no account. Adding an app to your library counts as one add whatever that setting says, and removing it the same day takes the add back. Nothing is counted for a Private app or a dedicated server, and your launches and adds of your own apps aren't counted.

**Results on GitHub:** If a maker switches on Results on GitHub for their app, then while it is on, the app is in the store and we offer Results on GitHub, we send GitHub SLAPorSLOP's result for the releases they send after switching it on, attached to each release's commit: a status named SLAPorSLOP with one fixed sentence and a link to the app's page. Anyone who can see the commit on GitHub sees it. It says nothing about players. After it is switched off or the app leaves the store, the only status we still send is one that replaces a "Passed" when no release on that commit is approved any more, and never for a Private app. GitHub keeps these, and we can't remove them, also after that maker closes their account.

**Browser games:** Play opens the game on its maker's own website. That site is run by the maker, not SLAPorSLOP, and its own privacy policy applies to what you do there. SLAPorSLOP doesn't send it your account or any data about you.

## Transfers
SLAPorSLOP is a US company. Transfers from the EU and UK rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK Extension), with standard contractual clauses as a fallback.

## Your rights
You can see, download, correct and delete your data.
- **Download:** "Download my data" on your account page gives you a copy of everything linked to your account.
- **Delete:** "Close my account" on the same page. What's kept, and why, is listed there and in the Terms.
- You can also object to processing based on legitimate interest, and complain to your data-protection authority. In the UK, you can complain to us first.
- We answer other requests within one month.

## How long we keep it
Only as long as needed: sessions for up to 180 days, job conversations for 30 days after closing, payment and tax records for 10 years, moderation records for 6 years. Open requests on People are kept for 90 days after they close, and the names put forward for a request as long as the request; a request that we hid is kept for the six months you have to appeal and one more month, or longer while an appeal about it is still waiting. If a news post or an open request is reported, its text is kept with the report. News a maker posts is kept until the maker removes it, the app is deleted or the maker's account is closed; a post that we hid is kept for the six months the maker has to appeal and one more month, or longer while an appeal about it is still waiting, then deleted. Daily totals per app hold no account and are kept while the app is on SLAPorSLOP.
