IronPoint Development LLC ("Slop") is the controller of the personal data described here.

## What we collect, and why

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Your display name, email (from your sign-in provider) and country | Your account, product emails, and choosing the rules for your country | Contract |
| Your sign-in provider's account ID | Signing you in. After you close your account, we keep it only to block the sign-in | Contract; legitimate interest (stopping closed or banned accounts returning) |
| Sessions and devices | Keeping you signed in; showing you where you're signed in | Contract |
| Your library, installs and playtime (if you share it) | Your library, "playing now" numbers, and weighting votes | Contract; legitimate interest |
| Votes | Ratings. Public on your profile by default; you can make them private | Contract; legitimate interest |
| Recommendations | "For you", from your library, playtime and votes. Switch it off on your account page | Legitimate interest |
| People profiles, jobs, agreements, payments and reviews | Running the marketplace and keeping the job record | Contract; legal obligation (tax) |
| Job conversations | Kept while open and 30 days after closing, then deleted; fingerprints kept to prove copies | Contract; legitimate interest |
| Reports, moderation decisions and appeals | Acting on reports and keeping the records the law requires (6 years) | Legal obligation; legitimate interest |
| Which version of each agreement you accepted | Proof of the contract (the life of your account plus 6 years) | Legal obligation; legitimate interest |

We never sell your data, and we don't use it to train AI models.

## Who receives it
Our hosting provider, GitHub (sign-in, apps' repositories), and Stripe (payments, as an independent controller). Makers never learn who donated unless the donor chooses to tell them.

## Transfers
Slop is a US company. Transfers from the EU and UK rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK Extension), with standard contractual clauses as a fallback.

## Your rights
You can see, download, correct and delete your data.
- **Download:** "Download my data" on your account page gives you a copy of everything linked to your account.
- **Delete:** "Close my account" on the same page. What's kept, and why, is listed there and in the Terms.
- You can also object to processing based on legitimate interest, and complain to your data-protection authority. In the UK, you can complain to us first.
- We answer other requests within one month.

## How long we keep it
Only as long as needed: sessions for up to 180 days, job conversations for 30 days after closing, payment and tax records for 10 years, moderation records for 6 years.
